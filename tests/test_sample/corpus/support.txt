Support hours and escalation

Support is staffed Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm UK time, excluding public
holidays. Tickets raised outside those hours are queued and answered on the next
working day.

Severity 1 incidents, defined as a complete loss of service in production, are
handled 24 hours a day through the on-call rota. The on-call number is issued
during onboarding and rotates quarterly.

Escalation to engineering happens automatically when a severity 1 ticket has
been open for more than two hours without a workaround.
