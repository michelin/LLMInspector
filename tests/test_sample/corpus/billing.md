# Billing and invoices

Invoices are issued on the first business day of each month and cover the
preceding calendar month. Payment terms are 30 days from the invoice date.

VAT receipts are downloadable from the Billing section of the account portal for
the previous 24 months. Older receipts must be requested from support.

## Overage charges

Usage beyond the contracted request volume is billed at the overage rate stated
in the order form. Overage is calculated monthly and never carried forward, so
an unusually heavy month does not affect subsequent invoices.
